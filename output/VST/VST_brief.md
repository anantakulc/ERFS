## Bull Case (3 Points)

1. **Guidance-exclusion stack worth $2–3bn in hidden EBITDA.** Cogentrix (5,500 MW gas, ~$700–800mn run-rate), Meta PPA deliveries (2,600 MW nuclear, begins late 2026), and Section 45U nuclear PTC (~$700–900mn/year) are all excluded from official 2026 guidance of $7.2bn. All three have signed legal instruments. If any two materialize as modeled, 2026 EBITDA prints above $8bn and 2027 approaches $10bn — versus a current EV that prices ~9x the guidance midpoint.

2. **Nuclear fleet is unreplicable and 59% contracted by 2034.** ~6,400 MWe across four plants (Comanche Peak, Beaver Valley, Davis-Besse, Perry) cannot be rebuilt on any practical timeline. AWS (1,200 MW, 20-year) and Meta (2,600 MW, 20-year) PPAs lock in estimated $85–100/MWh pricing. Investment-grade upgrade (S&P + Fitch to BBB-, March 2026) is repricing the cost of capital and the multiple — VST still trades at a 3–4x EV/EBITDA discount to Constellation (CEG) despite comparable nuclear quality.

3. **Analyst consensus of $225.29 (+62%) reflects a re-rating that hasn't happened yet.** 18 analysts, 14 Buy + 4 Strong Buy, zero Sells. Goldman Sachs $209, JPMorgan $231, BMO $244, Scotiabank $293. Q1 2026 EPS beat of 53.4% ($2.90 vs. $1.87 consensus) confirms the base business is already running ahead of street models before the exclusion stack is layered in.

## Bear Case

Vistra has grown net debt 77% in two years ($10.9bn to $19.3bn), FCF has collapsed 65% from its 2023 peak ($3.78bn to $1.32bn), and interest coverage has compressed to 2.1x — leaving no cushion if EBITDA disappoints. Three regulatory events represent simultaneous live threats: (1) FERC co-location rules expected ~June 18, 2026 could force full transmission charges on the AWS and Meta PPA structures, impairing their economics; (2) Section 45U nuclear PTC, worth $700–900mn/year, survived the House reconciliation vote but faces ongoing amendment risk in the Senate; (3) PJM capacity price collars are actively suppressing capacity revenue by ~18% versus unconstrained clearing, a headwind VST explicitly opposed. At $138.54, EV/2026E EBITDA of ~9.2x at the guidance midpoint offers no margin of safety — there is no tangible book value floor (negative at -$2.6bn). If two of five thesis pillars break simultaneously (FERC adverse + EBITDA miss, or 45U cap + leverage concern), the bear target range is $80–100/share, implying 28–42% downside. Precedent is NRG 2014–2016 (-71% in 18 months) and Calpine 2000–2002 (bankruptcy).
