# Eastroc Beverage (605499.SH / 09980.HK) — One-page brief
### "Why is the stock down despite strong financials?" · 2026-06-15

**Answer in one line:** The earnings are fine — the *multiple* reset. A stock that falls ~40% while EPS rises ~30% is, by arithmetic, multiple compression, triggered by growth deceleration on a high base and amplified by heavy insider selling, governance flags, and a weak HK debut.

**First, fix the number:** The raw "~60% crash" (¥339→¥132) is a trap — a 10-for-3 bonus + ¥25 dividend on 2026-05-11 accounts for most of it. **True split-adjusted drawdown ≈ 40–46%.**

**The fundamentals (the numerator is strong):**
- FY2025 revenue +31.8% (¥20.9bn), net profit +32.7% (¥4.42bn); Q1'26 +21.5% / +28.3%.
- Net margin 17%→21%; weighted ROE ~52%; #1 energy-drink share, 43%→48% by volume.
- Second curve real: electrolyte 补水啦 +119% to ¥3.3bn (15.7% of sales); energy reliance >90%→~75%.

**Why the price fell — ranked:**
1–2. **Multiple compression on growth deceleration (PRIMARY).** De-rated from ~40–54x (HK IPO) toward ~25x (~19x sector). Catalyst: core Dongpeng growth fell to +8.5% in Q4'25; group +32%→+21.5%. BofA cut EPS/target.
3. **Insider/family selling + float overhang (STRONG 2ND).** 60% lock-up freed May-2024; >¥7.4bn sold; 君正 fully exited; HK IPO broke issue, −23%.
4. **Governance discount (AMPLIFIER).** 存贷双高 (¥6.55bn short-term debt +119% alongside ¥10.5bn cash), 69% payout to the 67.7% Lin family, R&D ~0.4%.
5. **Competition** (Nongfu Spring on the second curve). 6. **China consumer macro** (background). 7. **HK dilution** (minor — modest EPS impact).

**One yellow flag:** OCF grew only ~7% vs net income +32.7% (rebate-funded shipment momentum); absolute cash flow still healthy. Monitor.

**Bull vs bear forward read:** Bull — PEG <1.0, cheapest since IPO, a de-rate to buy. Bear — ~25x vs ~19x sector with single-digit *core* growth, another 20–30% downside; selling/governance cap it.

**What flips it:** core re-accelerates to double-digits · second curve >30% of sales at >20% margin · insider net-selling stops · OCF re-converges with earnings.
