# Eastroc Beverage (Group) Co., Ltd. — 东鹏饮料
## Why the share price fell despite strong financials
**Ticker:** 605499.SH (Shanghai) · 09980.HK (Hong Kong, dual-listed 2026-02-03)
**Data as of:** 2026-06-15 · **Author:** Global Equity Research (Alpha cycle: Charlie bull ×2, Kilo bear ×2; numbers cross-corroborated across four independent streams)

---

## TL;DR — the disconnect, resolved

Eastroc is **not** falling *despite* good numbers — it is falling *because the market is repricing the multiple, not the earnings.* FY2025 revenue grew **+31.8%** and net profit **+32.7%**, yet the A-share is down **~40–46%** from its June-2025 peak (correctly split-adjusted). A price that falls while EPS rises ~30% is, almost by definition, **multiple compression** — and that is exactly what happened: the stock de-rated from a peak ~40–54x P/E toward ~25x (or ~18.6x on a split-adjusted basis), still a premium to its ~19x sector.

The trigger for the de-rate was **visible growth deceleration on a high base**; the amplifiers were **relentless insider/family share-selling**, **governance red flags**, and a **soft Hong Kong debut**.

> **Data-integrity note (a trap both sides flagged):** Raw quote feeds show ~¥339 (June 2025) → ~¥132 (June 2026), implying a ~60% crash. **This is wrong.** On 2026-05-11 the company executed its 2025 distribution — **¥25/10 shares cash + a 3-for-10 capitalization (10转3)** — so most of the ~¥200→¥132 move in May/June is an *ex-rights adjustment*, not a sell-off. Split-adjusted, the true peak-to-trough drawdown is **~40–46%**.

## 1. The fundamentals really are strong

| Metric (¥) | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY25 YoY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 8.51bn | 11.26bn | 15.84bn | **20.88bn** | **+31.8%** |
| Net income (归母) | 1.44bn | 2.04bn | 3.33bn | **4.42bn** | **+32.7%** |
| Gross margin | 42.3% | 43.1% | 44.8% | **44.9%** | +0.1pp |
| Net margin | 16.9% | 18.1% | 21.0% | **21.2%** | +0.2pp |

- **3-yr CAGR:** revenue ~34.8%, net income ~45.4%. Margins expanded ~430bp at the net line over three years.
- **Q1 2026:** revenue ¥5.888bn (**+21.5%**), net ¥1.257bn (**+28.3%**), gross margin 46.9% (+242bp YoY on lower PET/sugar costs).
- **Category leadership:** #1 in Chinese energy drinks by volume for four straight years; volume share **43.0% (2023) → 47.9% (2025)** (Nielsen IQ).
- **"Second growth curve":** electrolyte water 补水啦 +119% to ¥3.27bn (15.7% of revenue); other (coffee 大咖, tea 果之茶) +94%. Energy-drink reliance fell from >90% to ~75% of revenue.

This is a genuine category leader compounding ~30% with expanding margins. The earnings are not the problem.

## 2. Why the stock fell anyway — ranked drivers

### 1 & 2 (inseparable, PRIMARY) — Multiple compression triggered by growth deceleration
- The H-share IPO (Jan/Feb 2026) was priced at **~40–54x trailing P/E**. By March 2026 the A-share dynamic P/E had compressed to **~25.6x**, the H-share ~24x — vs a food-&-beverage **sector at ~19.4x**.
- The catalyst: **growth visibly rolled over on a high base.** Core Dongpeng Special Drink single-quarter growth collapsed to **+8.5% in Q4'25** (from +19% a year earlier); group revenue growth decelerated FY25 +31.8% → Q1'26 +21.5%. BofA cut its A-share target to ¥280 and trimmed FY26/27 EPS by 4%/5%.
- **Mechanically:** a ~40% price fall against +30% EPS growth = the multiple resetting from ~40x toward ~25x. This is the heart of the story. The market is repricing a "20%-grower," not a "40%-grower."

### 3 (STRONG SECONDARY) — Insider / family selling and float overhang
- A **60.18% lock-up (241m shares, ~¥53.4bn) unlocked in May 2024.** Insiders have since realized **>¥7.4bn**; the second-largest holder 君正投资 **fully exited (>¥4bn)**; PE holder 鲲鹏 trimmed; the founder's son cashed ~¥2bn. The drawdown began as this selling concentrated. Insiders selling a "cheap" stock is a loud tell.
- The **Feb-2026 Hong Kong IPO** (~40.9m H-shares at HK$248) **nearly broke issue on day one** and fell **~23% to ~HK$190** by April — adding float and leaving the marginal new investor underwater and unwilling to pay up.

### 4 (AMPLIFIER) — Governance discount: why it de-rates *below* peers
- **存贷双高 (high cash AND high debt):** ~¥10.5bn cash/financial assets alongside short-term borrowings that ballooned **+118.7% to ¥6.55bn**, while paying out **~69% of profit as dividends** — of which the **67.7%-controlling Lin family** takes the lion's share. Borrowing while paying large dividends to the controlling family reads as cash extraction and earns a governance haircut.
- **R&D is only ~0.4% of revenue** — thin credibility for a "platform beverage company" pivoting into categories dominated by better-capitalized rivals.

### 5 (CONTRIBUTING) — Competition on the second curve
- The growth bridge (电解质水/tea) now runs straight into **Nongfu Spring**, which launched its own electrolyte product under its flagship brand. 补水啦 growth already decelerated to ~+13% in Q1'26 on a high base.

### 6 (BACKGROUND) — China consumer macro / A-share staples de-rating
- Sector P/E ~19.4x sets the gravitational pull, and broad consumer caution is the tide — but Eastroc fell **far more than the sector**, so its drawdown is **company-specific**, not just macro.

### 7 (MINOR / OVERSTATED) — HK dual-listing dilution
- The ~40.9m H-shares are a small fraction of ~520m total shares; direct **EPS dilution is modest**. The H-listing matters as a *sentiment/float* event (weak debut), not as dilution math.

## 3. The one real quality-of-earnings flag (reconciled)
The bull noted "OCF ¥6.17bn comfortably exceeds net income"; the bear noted "OCF grew only ~6.6%." **Both are true.** Absolute operating cash flow is healthy (¥5.79bn → ¥6.17bn) but its *growth* badly lagged net income's +32.7%, and distributor prepayments fell while sales-rebate liabilities rose sharply — a sign some shipment momentum is rebate-funded. This is a **yellow flag to monitor**, not yet a red one.

## 4. Bull vs. bear — where they agree and differ
- **Agree:** the fall is a multiple reset, not an earnings miss; deceleration was the catalyst; insider supply is a real overhang; the raw ~60% drawdown number is a split-adjustment trap (true ~40%).
- **Differ — the forward read:**
  - **Bull:** ~15x forward / ~25x trailing on ~30% growth = **PEG < 1.0**, the cheapest since IPO, with a fresh HK$10bn war chest and ~2.9% dividend yield — a de-rate to buy.
  - **Bear:** ~25x vs a ~19x sector with single-digit *core* growth = **not finished compressing**; base-case another 20–30% downside, governance/selling cap the stock.

---

## 5. What would change the picture
- **Re-acceleration of the core** (Dongpeng Special Drink back to sustained double-digit growth) — the single most important swing factor.
- **Second curve scaling to >30% of revenue at >20% margins** while holding off Nongfu Spring.
- **Insider/family net selling stops** (watch SSE 减持 filings) and the H-share float clears without further supply shock.
- **Cash flow re-converges with earnings** (OCF/NI back toward parity; rebate dependency stabilizes).

If those hold, the de-rate was just mean-reversion on a healthy franchise and ~18–25x is a floor. If the core keeps decelerating, every soft quarter justifies a lower multiple.

---

## 6. Historical precedent
The pattern — single hero SKU + maturing category + premium multiple + heavy insider ownership + post-lock-up supply — is the classic Chinese consumer template. Bears explicitly invoke **养元饮品 / Yangyuan (六个核桃, 603156.SH)** ("the next Yangyuan") and the post-trademark-dispute stagnation of **Red Bull China**. The global analog is **Celsius (CELH)**, which fell ~70% from its 2024 high when single-product energy-drink growth decelerated and channel inventory normalized — same DNA: one hero SKU, peak share, peak multiple, air-pocket.
