## 1. Company Overview

Eastroc Beverage is the maker of **Dongpeng Special Drink (东鹏特饮)**, China's #2 energy drink brand by volume. Founded in Shenzhen in 1987, listed May 2021 on the Shanghai STAR Market. Core SKU: the iconic 250ml can priced at ~RMB 3.5–5, a deliberate price-point discount to Red Bull China (RMB 6+), targeting blue-collar workers, truck drivers, construction workers, and students.

**Revenue breakdown (2024E):**

| Segment | Share |
|---|---|
| Energy drinks (Dongpeng Special Drink) | ~90% |
| Other beverages (electrolyte water, Dongpeng 0 Sugar, tea) | ~10% |

**Distribution:** ~3,000+ distributors, ~3.5–4 million retail touch-points nationally. Originally a Guangdong powerhouse; national expansion has been the primary growth story since 2021.

## 4. Bull Case

- **National white space:** Guangdong is saturated but North/Northwest/Northeast China remain underpenetrated — per-capita consumption in those regions is <30% of Guangdong levels.
- **Category still small:** China energy drink per-capita consumption is ~1/10th of the US. Secular tailwind.
- **Premiumization optionality:** New SKUs (Dongpeng 0 Sugar, larger formats at RMB 6–8) can lift average selling price without cannibalizing the core.
- **Operating leverage:** SG&A as % of revenue should fall as the fixed cost of national distribution is already being built.
- **Strong FCF / capital return:** Growing buyback & dividend program supports a floor on valuation.
- **Bull target:** RMB 160–180 (25–28x 2025E EPS of ~RMB 6.5)

---

## 5. Bear Case

- **Red Bull China is not dead.** Despite years of trademark litigation, Huabin Group continues to manufacture and sell Red Bull in China under a contested license. If the legal dispute resolves in Huabin's favor, Red Bull could re-accelerate marketing spend — directly pressuring Eastroc's share gains.
- **Single-SKU dependency.** ~90% revenue from one product. One food-safety incident, labeling ban, or caffeine-content regulation = existential risk.
- **White-label commoditization.** Dozens of regional energy drink brands now exist at RMB 2–3. Eastroc's RMB 3.5 positioning is being squeezed from below.
- **China consumer deflation.** Discretionary staples are under pressure; trading down is real.
- **Growth deceleration is inevitable.** At RMB 15bn revenue, the law of large numbers means 30%+ growth cannot persist.
- **Bear target:** RMB 80–100 (15–17x bear-case 2025E EPS of ~RMB 5.5)

## 7. Why Share Price Declined Despite Strong Financials

The disconnect boils down to one equation:

**Share Price = EPS × P/E Multiple**

Eastroc's EPS has grown ~3x since IPO. But its P/E multiple has compressed ~4x. The multiple fell faster than earnings rose — so the stock is lower.

### 7.1 The Starting Valuation Was Irrational (The Original Sin)

At IPO in May 2021, Eastroc traded up to **70–80x trailing P/E**. The market was pricing in:
- 30%+ growth every year for a decade
- Zero competitive response
- Zero macro risk

When any of those assumptions frayed, the multiple didn't drop to 60x — it dropped to 25x. A company can grow earnings 150% and still be a bad stock if you paid 80x for it.

### 7.2 China's Macro Became a Structural Headwind

- **Property crisis:** Household wealth is ~70% real estate in China. Evergrande collapse → consumers felt poorer and spent less.
- **Youth unemployment:** Peaked above 20% in 2023 — Eastroc's core demographic (young male blue-collar workers) was directly affected.
- **Deflation:** When consumers expect prices to fall, they delay purchases. Toxic for branded goods premiumization.
- **COVID zero policy (2022):** Shut down on-trade and convenience channels that Eastroc depends on.

### 7.3 Growth Deceleration Is What Markets Actually Punish

| Year | Revenue Growth | Market Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 | +40% | Euphoria → stock surges |
| 2022 | +22% | Disappointment → de-rating begins |
| 2023 | +37% | Recovery, but consensus expected 40%+ |
| 2024E | ~+29% | Deceleration narrative cements |

The market doesn't care if you grew 29%. It cares that you grew *less than expected* and *less than before*. A decelerating growth rate signals maturity — and mature companies don't deserve growth multiples.

### 7.4 Competition Changed the Narrative

When Eastroc IPO'd, the bull thesis was simple: *"Red Bull is tied up in litigation, Eastroc will take the #2 slot and march to #1."* That narrative was complicated by:
- Red Bull China (Huabin) never stopped selling
- Nongfu Spring launched SCREAM — a well-funded, premium-positioned competitor
- Monster doubled down on its Coca-Cola distribution agreement in China
- Dozens of white-label energy drinks at RMB 2–3 squeezing from below

### 7.5 Mechanical Selling Pressure (Technical, Not Fundamental)

Three waves of selling had nothing to do with business quality:
1. **6-month lock-up expiry (Nov 2021):** Early employees and Series A investors exited
2. **12-month lock-up (May 2022):** Strategic investors exited
3. **36-month lock-up (May 2024):** Largest block — controlling family could reduce stake

### 7.6 The Compounding Math Works Against You at High Multiples

| | 2021 (IPO peak) | 2025E |
|---|---|---|
| EPS (RMB) | ~3.0 | ~6.5 |
| EPS growth | — | +117% |
| P/E multiple | 75x | 22x |
| **Share price** | **~225** | **~143** |

EPS more than doubled. The stock is still down ~36% from peak. That is 100% multiple compression. The business won. The stock lost — because someone paid 75x for it.

## 8. Investment Conclusion

**Rating: HOLD / Selective BUY on weakness**

The fundamentals are genuinely strong — Eastroc is a real business with pricing power, strong FCF, and a long runway in China's underpenetrated interior markets. The bear case is not about the business failing; it's about whether the current ~22–25x P/E is the right multiple for a single-market, single-SKU consumer company operating in a deflationary macro environment with intensifying competition.
